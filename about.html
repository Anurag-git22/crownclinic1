Patient Safety First

Hospital-Grade Sterilization Protocols

At Crown Care Dental Clinic, patient safety is non-negotiable. We follow strict international hygiene standards to eliminate any risk of cross-contamination.

Swipe protocols →

Class-B Autoclave

Medical-grade high-pressure steam sterilization eliminates 100% of bacterial and viral spores from all surgical tools and handpieces.

Sealed Pouches

Instruments are hermetically sealed in autoclave indicator pouches that are opened directly in front of each patient.

Surface Disinfection

Dental operatory chairs, light handles, and working countertops undergo hospital-grade disinfectant wipes between every visit.

Single-Use Disposables

Gloves, masks, patient bibs, suction tips, and disposable barrier sleeves are discarded immediately after each procedure.
