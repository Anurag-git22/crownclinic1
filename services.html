Comprehensive Dental Treatments & Procedures

From preventive teeth cleaning and single-visit root canals to advanced dental implants and smile makeovers, our clinical care is tailored for lasting oral wellness.

Endodontics

Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Save natural teeth infected by deep cavities or trauma. Performed using modern motorized rotary instrumentation and local anesthetics to ensure a virtually pain-free experience, often completed in a single comfortable sitting.

Indications: Severe throbbing toothache, sensitivity to hot foods, swelling near gums.

Benefit: Preserves natural jawbone & natural chewing alignment.

Pain-Free Tech
Implantology

Dental Implants

The gold standard in tooth replacement. Biocompatible titanium posts fuse directly with your jawbone, topped with lifelike custom ceramic crowns that look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth.

Indications: Single or multiple missing teeth, loose dentures, broken roots.

Benefit: Prevents jawbone shrinkage, restores 100% bite strength.

Permanent Solution
Prosthodontics

Crown & Bridge

Custom fabricated Zirconia, E-Max, and porcelain-fused caps designed with digital precision to restore structural strength after root canals or bridge gaps caused by missing teeth.

Indications: Post-RCT protection, fractured enamel, cosmetic reshaping.

Benefit: High chip resistance, shade-matched to natural teeth.

Zirconia & Ceramic
Preventive

Oral Prophylaxis (Cleaning)

Gentle ultrasonic scaling eliminates hardened tartar (calculus) and tea/coffee stains that normal brushing cannot reach. Completed with micro-polishing for a smooth, refreshing mouth feel.

Indications: Bleeding gums, bad breath (halitosis), yellow surface deposits.

Benefit: Reverses early gingivitis and prevents tooth mobility.

Every 6 Months
Cosmetic

Teeth Whitening

In-office bleaching formulated with enamel-safe hydrogen peroxide gels activated by specialized light. Lightens deep discoloration and delivers dazzling brightness for upcoming celebrations or weddings.

Indications: Yellowed teeth, tobacco/food stains, aging discoloration.

Benefit: 5-8 shades brighter in a single 45-minute sitting.

Instant Results
Pediatric Care

Child Dental Care

Gentle, empathetic dentistry designed specifically for infants, children, and young teens. Includes fluoride varnishes, pulpectomy, milk tooth restorations, and habit counseling in a playful, comforting setting.

Indications: Milk tooth decay, thumb sucking, cavity prevention.

Benefit: Eliminates dental phobia early in childhood.

Zero-Fear Clinic
Oral Surgery

Tooth Extraction & Wisdom Tooth

Minimally traumatic extractions for non-restorable teeth or impacted third molars. Carried out under profound local anesthesia with specialized micro-elevators to protect the surrounding jawbone.

Indications: Impacted wisdom tooth pain, severe decay, crowding.

Benefit: Fast healing with detailed post-operative instructions.

Atraumatic
Restorative

Composite Fillings & Sealants

Tooth-colored nano-hybrid composite resins that blend seamlessly with natural enamel. We also offer preventive pit & fissure sealants for deep grooves in molars to stop food entrapment and cavities.

Indications: Cavity spots, food lodgement, old silver filling replacement.

Benefit: 100% mercury-free, natural tooth aesthetics.

Tooth-Colored
Aesthetic

Bonding & Teeth Reshaping

Direct artistic application of composite resin to repair minor chipped edges, fill unsightly gaps between front teeth, or gently sculpt uneven enamel edges into a symmetrical smile.

Indications: Chipped front tooth, midline diastema (gap), uneven tooth length.

Benefit: Immediate cosmetic transformation in a single session.

Quick Fix
Smile Design

Veneers & Smile Design

Ultra-thin, custom porcelain or composite shells bonded to the front surface of teeth. Designed to correct chronic staining, minor crowding, or irregular shapes to give you a camera-ready smile.

Indications: Stubborn fluorosis stains, worn edges, crooked appearance.

Benefit: Stain-resistant, natural light-reflecting porcelain.

Hollywood Smile
Senior Care

Dentures & Bridges

Custom fabricated flexible partial dentures (Valplast) and complete acrylic dentures designed for superior retention, comfortable suction, and natural lip support for older adults.

Indications: Full arch missing teeth, multiple missing molars.

Benefit: Lightweight, flexible, crack-resistant materials.

Custom Fit
Diagnostics

Check-ups & Digital X-Ray

Thorough oral cancer screening, periodontal pocket assessment, and instant high-definition digital sensor (RVG) imaging with minimal radiation to diagnose interdental decay and root health.

Indications: Routine 6-month checkup, unclear tooth pain, pre-treatment planning.

Benefit: Instant on-screen display to see your own dental condition.

Low Radiation
Urgent Care

Emergency Dental Care

Prompt emergency relief for acute toothache, knocked-out teeth from sporting trauma, fractured crowns, facial swelling, or uncontrolled gum bleeding. Priority same-day slot allocation.

Indications: Unbearable pain keeping you awake, traumatic facial impact.

Benefit: Quick pain relief and tooth stabilization.

Periodontics

Gum Therapy & Periodontics

Specialized subgingival curettage and root planing to eliminate deep microbial colonies causing chronic gum recession, bleeding while brushing, and shaky teeth.

Indications: Loose teeth, persistent bad breath, tender swollen gums.

Benefit: Firms up gum attachment to prevent early tooth loss.

Gum Rejuvenation
Prevention

Preventive Dentistry & Nightguards

Custom-molded occlusal nightguards to shield your teeth from nocturnal grinding (bruxism) and TMJ pain. Preventive mouthguards for athletes to avoid traumatic tooth fractures.

Indications: Morning jaw soreness, worn flat teeth, teeth grinding sounds.

Benefit: Relieves morning migraines and preserves tooth enamel.

Enamel Shield
